Operational Briefing

Welcome to the Winter Weather Decision Simulator. You are serving as the Regional Transit & Operations Director for NMDOT, managing key interstate corridors during winter weather events.

The Hazard Threshold: 4 Inches

In your district's winter maintenance plan, 4 inches of snow accumulation is the critical operational tipping point:

  • Below 4": Traffic friction and routine plowing keep lanes open without major pre-treatment mobilization.
  • At or Above 4": Heavy pack-snow bonds directly to cold asphalt, causing severe traction failure, jackknifed semi-trucks, and major corridor closures unless highways are pre-treated.

The Economic Model ($C/L = 20\%$)

  • Pre-Treat Highways ($2,000): Prevents snow bonding and eliminates transit failure risk. This expenditure is non-refundable if snowfall stays below 4".
  • Hold Operations ($0): Preserves operational budget. However, if snowfall reaches 4"+ without pre-treatment, unmitigated road ice causes severe crashes and emergency response costs ($10,000).
